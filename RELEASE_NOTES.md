Download an installer for your computer: .deb or AppImage for Linux, .exe for Windows, and the matching .dmg for Intel or Apple Silicon Macs.

Version 0.1.10 adds donation, Discord support and community, and collaboration email links to the new-tab page and Settings. The blank-tab fix is included. Existing encrypted local accounts remain compatible. Local account creation, sign-in, Lock/sign-out, and encrypted saved profiles are included. Use a password of at least 12 characters. Accounts stay on this computer; there is no cloud sync or password recovery. Website sessions end on sign-out or restart. Downloaded files remain ordinary files outside the encrypted profile.

SHA256SUMS.txt contains checksums for the five installers. GitHub's automatically generated Source code archives contain this download page and distribution workflow, not the browser installer or application source.
